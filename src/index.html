      04 / BEFORE YOU RELEASE

      Pre-release checklist

      Mark the steps you’ve done for this order. Progress is not a payment result or a recommendation to release anything.

      Warning signs

      Pause and check independently. These signs are not findings of fraud.

      • Pressure to release goods or work before confirmation.
      • Screenshots instead of a transaction you can independently check.
      • A transaction ID that cannot be found on the selected network.
      • A different amount from what you agreed.
      • A receiving address that does not match yours.
      • A sender who insists an unconfirmed payment is final.
      • Demands for secrecy or unusual urgency.

      Privacy and security

      Your profile, preferences and saved addresses stay in this browser. Verification results are saved here only when you choose Save this check. Browser storage is not encrypted or synced.

      The public explorer sees your connection details and requested addresses or transaction IDs. It receives a signed test transaction only if you choose to broadcast.

      The temporary Signet wallet keeps its private key in this tab’s memory. Reloading or closing the tab loses access to any remaining test coins. YOU never sends or stores the key. Never enter a mainnet seed phrase: it controls real funds, and YOU does not need it or support Mainnet.

      About YOU

      Version 1.0.0

      YOU helps Nigerian freelancers and small sellers check Bitcoin payments on test networks. It cannot detect fraud, confirm a bank payment or recover a production wallet. Demand has not been validated.

      What this cannot prove

      A Bitcoin check does not prove a naira transfer cleared, who sent the payment, or whether a screenshot is authentic. Unconfirmed payments may be replaced even when explicit RBF signalling is not detected.

      Saved checks

      Saved only in this browser. Not synced. Clearing your browser data removes them.

      How YOU handles your data

      No accounts, analytics or stored spending keys. YOU saves a verification result locally only when you choose Save this check. The public mempool.space explorer sees your IP address, requested transaction IDs, wallet address on a balance lookup and any signed sweep you broadcast. If the explorer is unavailable, YOU does not give a payment decision. Not for real funds.
