# Nigerian Bitcoin community poll and feedback form

## Ready-to-post poll

Hi, I’m a solo student doing early research on how people in Nigeria check Bitcoin payments for work or goods. If you’re comfortable, could you answer these three questions? “Not applicable” is fine. Please don’t share any names, wallet details, transaction IDs, screenshots or bank details.

1. Have you accepted Bitcoin directly for work or goods in Nigeria?
2. How do you currently check that a payment arrived?
3. Would a tool that checks the receiving address, exact amount and confirmations help? What would it need to do to be useful?

Ask moderators before posting. Public replies show handles; don’t copy those handles into research notes. No poll has been posted and no responses have been collected.

## Short feedback form description

**Title: How do you check a Bitcoin payment?** This is voluntary early research for a student project for Nigerian freelancers and sellers. Please describe your usual method, not an actual payment. Do not include names, emails, wallet addresses, transaction IDs, seed phrases, screenshots or bank details. You may skip any question. Feedback helps me decide what to keep or change; it is not evidence of how all sellers work.

Use the same three questions: Q1 multiple choice (Yes / No / Prefer not to say), Q2 short text, Q3 short text. Turn off email collection and sign-in requirements. Keep response summaries private. Test a signed-out link before replacing the app’s explicitly labelled unpublished-form placeholder.
